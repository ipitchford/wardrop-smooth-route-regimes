# Sioux Falls test-network data (not redistributed)

`code/sioux_falls.py` reads `SiouxFalls_net.tntp` and `SiouxFalls_trips.tntp` from this directory. They are not included: the Transportation Networks for Research repository states that its data sets are for academic research purposes only and must be cited, and it grants no redistribution licence. Fetch them with `python3 tools/fetch_siouxfalls.py`, which downloads them from commit d1639b4ef218c17928ba573e806ddf8ba5e7ae6d and checks their SHA-256.

Source: Transportation Networks for Research Core Team, *Transportation Networks for Research*, https://github.com/bstabler/TransportationNetworks (accessed 22 October 2026); network from LeBlanc, Morlok and Pierskalla, Transportation Research 9 (1975) 309-318.
