Source of both questions: Cominetti, Dose and Scarsini, *Phase transitions of the price-of-anarchy function in multi-commodity routing games*, Transportation Research Part B 182, 102922 (2024), doi:10.1016/j.trb.2024.102922; open version arXiv:2305.03459v2, whose numbering is used (Question 1: paragraph after Remark 3.5; Question 2: immediately before Example 4.9).

* **Proved in writing:** Theorem 3.1 (fixed-regime C¹ link flows for C¹ costs with c'(z) > 0 for z > 0, interior demands, any network); Proposition 3.3 (general endpoint extension fails); Theorem 4.1 and Corollary 4.2 (weak slope comparison for BPR costs, expansions and contractions, zero free-flow allowed); Theorem 4.5 (strict slope jumps for almost every free-flow vector at nondegenerate nested breakpoints); the three-link and five-link examples of Section 5 (E, P ∈ C⁴ \ C⁵); Theorem 5.3 (m + 2 links, C^(2m−2) \ C^(2m−1)); Theorem 6.1 with Appendix B (five links minimal for a common exponent); Theorem 7.4 (optimum-side transition: S ∈ C^(2m−1) \ C^(2m) at t = 1/γ, and the same for P when E is C^(2m) there, checked exactly for the quartic example); Section 7 perturbation and demand-direction results; Appendix A (several contacts in the two-bank architecture).
* **Exact computation** (rational or symbolic, confirming constants and instances, not the general theorems): `code/verify_exact.py`, `code/verify_small_examples.py`, `code/verify_optimum_transition.py` (with an exact interval check), the `checks/` programs and the finite enumeration in `checks/small_networks/verify_minimality.py`.
* **Numerical:** the size of the P⁽⁶⁾ jump at the optimum-side transition (about +89.7); the practice numbers of Section 8 (50–60-digit mpmath computations with the physical costs, agreeing with the exact series) and the five-link figure.
* **Exploratory:** the Sioux Falls scan (Section 8.5), one test network and one artificial demand path.
* **Open:** the BPR endpoint case at zero demand; genericity at degenerate breakpoints; four versus five links for mixed-exponent expansions; smoothness at non-nested breakpoints; prevalence of near-tangent activations in calibrated networks.

Scope: static deterministic Wardrop equilibrium with separable costs. The minimality theorem covers costs a_e + b_e z^p with a_e, b_e > 0 and one common p > 1, all simple paths, proportional positive demands and nested breakpoints. No proof assistant was used.
